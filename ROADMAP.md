# Roadmap

Tetrix is at 0.6.0 (2026-10-28): it plays well solo and on a LAN, and the
rest of the planned game is still to build. 1.0.0 is when everything below
is in.

## 0.6.0: built

- [x] Title menu; solo play against up to five bots at four strengths
- [x] LAN play for up to six: automatic discovery, a lobby, bots in empty seats
- [x] The full TetriNET special set, classic lines, level averaging
- [x] Win list across rounds
- [x] Juice pass; a layout that scales with the terminal
- [x] Direct join across networks (`tetrix --join <id>`)

## 0.7.0: playing together

- [ ] Play on two physical machines on a real LAN, including a Wi-Fi network
  that blocks broadcast
- [ ] Partyline: chat in the lobby, and in the game (`t`)
- [ ] Teams

## 0.8.0: the end of a round

- [ ] Sudden death: after 600 s, a line every 10 s (as jetrix does)
- [ ] Spectating for eliminated players

## 0.9.0: the other two modes

- [ ] Dr Mario: pills and viruses on the same engine
- [ ] Puyo Puyo: blob pairs and chains

## 1.0.0

- [ ] Finding games over the internet, not only on the LAN (waits on how
  Asciiverse hosts internet play)
- [ ] Everything above played through and tuned

## After 1.0

- [ ] Mixed mode: each player picks Tetrix, Dr Mario or Puyo Puyo, and
  attacks cross between them. Needs its own design pass first.
